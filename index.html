Questions

Everything Else

What should I wear?

Garden formal. Long dresses, barong, or a light suit. It is a lawn at dusk, so the grass beats stilettos every time.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation code holds a fixed number of seats, and the RSVP form will only let you choose up to that number. If you think we have made a mistake, message us before you reply.

Are children welcome?

Only the children named on your invitation, and they are counted in your reserved seats. There is a children's table with its own dessert menu — tell us their ages in the message field.

Where can we stay?

We are holding rooms at two hotels within ten minutes of the venue. Reply "hotels" to your confirmation email and we will send the booking codes.

What is the weather like in September?

Cool for the Philippines, around 20°C after sunset and breezy on the ridge. Bring a light layer.

Can I post photos?

Please do, after the ceremony. During the vows we would rather see faces than phones.

A Few Kindly Reminders

🤍RSVP deadline

Kindly submit your RSVP on or before August 30, 2026. Responses received after this date may not be accommodated.

👥By invitation only

Each invitation is reserved exclusively for the guest(s) named on it. We respectfully ask that no additional guests be brought unless previously approved by the couple.

👶Children

To help us manage our seating arrangements, only children specifically included in the invitation are invited to attend.

👗Dress code

Please join us in semi-formal to formal attire. We kindly ask guests to avoid wearing white, ivory, or cream out of respect for the bride.

⏰Arrival time

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony so everyone is comfortably seated before the processional begins.

📸Ceremony etiquette

We warmly invite you to be fully present during our ceremony. Kindly silence your phones and allow our photographers to capture the moment.

🚗Parking

Complimentary parking will be available at the venue. Additional details can be found in the Venue section.

💌Special requests

If you have dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or other important concerns, please let us know in the message section of the RSVP form.
